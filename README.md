2. BUSINESS OVERVIEW

==================================================

LedgerAssure AI is a UK-based AI-powered software platform designed for the UK's smallest businesses.

The platform is designed to automate and simplify:

- Bookkeeping

- Making Tax Digital compliance

- Tax-related financial preparation

- Continuous assurance for businesses that need it

- ESG/carbon reporting where required

The target users are primarily:

- Sole traders

- Micro-businesses

- Owner-managed businesses

- Businesses with 0–9 employees

- Businesses without an in-house finance/accounting function

The business plan identifies approximately 5.3 million UK businesses with 0–9 employees as the core addressable segment.

The problem LedgerAssure AI is solving:

Small-business owners often have two difficult choices:

1. Manage bookkeeping and compliance manually, which can be slow, stressful and error-prone.

OR

2. Pay for multiple disconnected accounting, bookkeeping, tax, audit-readiness and sustainability tools/services.

These separate systems require data to be manually entered, reconciled and transferred between tools.

LedgerAssure AI is designed to bring these processes together through one connected evidence-based system.

The central concept is:

"Capture financial information once and continuously turn it into compliant, evidence-backed financial information."

==================================================

The most important product concept on the website must be the:

UNIFIED EVIDENCE LEDGER

This is the architectural foundation of LedgerAssure AI.

Explain it in simple language for a non-technical visitor.

The Unified Evidence Ledger is a single, versioned data structure that connects:

SOURCE RECORD

5. TECHNICAL MECHANISM — PRESENT IN SIMPLE LANGUAGE

==================================================

The business plan describes three major technical elements.

A. DATA INGESTION AND NORMALISATION

The planned platform is designed to connect with:

- Business bank accounts through FCA-authorised Open Banking APIs

- Accounting software where applicable

- Invoices

- Receipts

The system is designed to capture and normalise financial information.

B. AI-ASSISTED TRANSACTION CATEGORISATION

An off-the-shelf Large Language Model is intended to assist with transaction categorisation.

The system is NOT described as training its own proprietary foundation model.

The categorisation system is designed to include confidence scoring.

If the system has low confidence in a classification, the user is expected to review and confirm it.

This human-confirmation mechanism is important and should be presented as a trust/safety feature.

C. VERSION-CONTROLLED COMPLIANCE RULES ENGINE

A separate rules engine is designed to apply relevant:

- Making Tax Digital requirements

- VAT requirements

- Income Tax requirements

The rules engine checks financial information before filing preparation.
